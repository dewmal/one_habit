Privacy policy

App: One Habit (com.syigen.onehabit) · Developer: Syigen · Effective: 15 November 2026

The short version

  • There is no One Habit account. You never sign up or log in.
  • Your plan, your daily log, your health-check answers and your full chat history stay on your phone, in an encrypted database.
  • When you chat with the coach, a small part of the conversation is sent over the internet to an AI model so it can write a reply.
  • The app has no ads and no analytics, and we do not sell data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your profile: language, quiet hours and notification limit
  • Your habit goal, starting level and plan
  • Your daily log: what you did and how it felt
  • Your answers to the health-check questions
  • Your full chat history

We cannot see any of this. It is never uploaded to us.

What leaves your phone

The coach is an AI. To write a reply, the app sends a request through our gateway server to a third-party AI model provider. A request can contain:

  • The last few messages of the open chat, including what you just typed
  • A short summary of your plan and progress
  • If the health check asked for a gentle plan, only that result ("keep very gentle"), not your answers
  • A random ID the app creates when it is installed. It is not linked to your name, phone number, Google account or any other identity

If you type something about your health in the chat, such as "my knee hurts", that message is part of the request.

The app does not send your name, contact details, location, contacts, photos or files.

How this data is used

  • Chat content and the summary are used only to write the coach's reply. Our gateway passes the request on and does not store it. Its logs hold only counts and error codes, never message content.
  • The random ID is used only to limit how many requests one phone can send, to stop abuse. It is kept in the gateway's memory for about one hour and is not saved.
  • The AI model provider processes the request for us to produce the reply. The provider may keep requests for a limited time under its own terms, for example to detect abuse.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell or rent data to anyone.

Health information

One Habit is a habit coach. It is not a medical service and does not diagnose or treat any condition. The health check is a few yes or no questions used only to keep your plan gentle. If you have a health concern, please talk to a doctor.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. They do not come from a server.

Google Drive backup (planned for a later version)

A later version will let you back up to your own Google Drive. It will be optional and off by default. The backup file is locked on your phone with a password you choose before it is uploaded, so neither Google nor we can read it. Google sign-in will be used only for this backup.

Security

  • The database on your phone is encrypted.
  • Requests to the gateway are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Keeping and deleting data

Data on your phone stays there until you delete it. The "My data" screen lets you view and delete anything. Uninstalling the app removes all of it. See Delete your data.

Children

One Habit is made for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@example.com

Delete your data

One Habit has no account and keeps your data on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open One Habit.
  2. Open the My data screen.
  3. Choose what to delete: one project, or everything.

Deleted data is removed from the phone. It cannot be brought back.

Delete everything by uninstalling

Uninstalling the app removes its database from your phone.

Data outside your phone

We do not keep a copy of your chats, plans or logs, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. The random ID used for the request limit is cleared from the gateway's memory after about one hour.

Target audience answers

Play Console > Policy > App content > Target audience and content. Play only opens this section after the Ads and App access sections are done.

QuestionAnswer
Target age group18 and over only
Could your store listing unintentionally appeal to children?No

Why 18 and over

  • The first habit is going to the gym, and the health check is written for adults.
  • Picking any age group under 13 puts the app under Google's Families policy, which adds strict rules.
  • It matches the privacy policy, which says the app is for adults.
Keep the listing consistent: do not use cartoon characters or child-like art in the icon and screenshots. Google can reject the "No" answer if the listing looks made for children.
